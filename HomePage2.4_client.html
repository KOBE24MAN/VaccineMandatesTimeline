About this timeline

MandEval

MandEval collected a structured dataset of COVID-19 vaccine mandate events so that researchers can compare how different policies developed across time and jurisdictions. This website turns that static dataset into an interactive public timeline that makes the key dates, jurisdictions, mandate types, and event details easier to explore.

Project team

This interactive prototype was built as part of a CITS5551 engineering design project at The University of Western Australia by Zhiheng Zhou, Zijun Zhou, Joel Fitzpatrick, Jiaren Zhu, and Yupeng Sun. The site was designed to support public viewing, client handover, and later hosting on client or university infrastructure.
